Expressive Zero-Shot Voice Cloning

Rethinking Post-Training for Expressive Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech

Zero-shot voice cloning typically emphasizes preserving the timbre and prosodic characteristics of a reference speaker. However, in real-world use, speech should adapt its delivery to the target text rather than remain tied to a fixed speaking style. In this work, we formulate this problem as text-adaptive prosody synthesis for zero-shot voice cloning and study how post-training can endow a cloning model with this capability.

Four-step post-training pipeline

  • Stage 1: SFT (Exp 0), DPO (Exp 1), and DPO (Exp 2) build the cold-start expressive baseline.
  • Stage 2: GSPO continues from the Stage 1 checkpoint and jointly optimizes prosody, quality, intelligibility, and similarity.
  • Stage 3: OPSD uses the GSPO checkpoint for on-policy self-distillation.

Framework overview

Post-training
Stage 1

Direct Preference Optimization

Creates a cold start by aligning the model with text-appropriate expressive speech, improving prosodic controllability before more complex optimization.

Stage 2

Group Sequence Policy Optimization

Jointly optimizes expressiveness, acoustic quality, intelligibility, and speaker similarity, stabilizing the end-to-end training signal.

Stage 3

On-Policy Self-Distillation

Transfers expressive behavior from strong-reference conditions to weak-reference conditions, enabling self-improvement without external labels.
